# 🥔 SOL-ValPotato

**SOL-ValPotato** is a comprehensive rewrite of the [Spice of Life: Valheim Edition](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/spice-of-life-valheim-edition) mod, preserving its core mechanics while enhancing compatibility and extensibility. Notably, it offers seamless integration with mods like [Some Assembly Required](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/some-assembly-required), enriching the culinary experience in Minecraft.

## 🧭 Overview

In vanilla Minecraft, the hunger system often feels like a minor inconvenience, easily mitigated by consuming large quantities of a single food item. SOL-ValPotato transforms this system by introducing a more strategic approach to sustenance:

* **Three Food Slots**: Inspired by Valheim, players have three food slots. Each consumed food item occupies a slot, providing specific health and regeneration benefits.
* **Timed Effects**: Food effects last for a set duration, during which the same food cannot be reconsumed, encouraging dietary diversity.
* **Strategic Eating**: Players are incentivized to combine different foods to maximize benefits, adding depth to the survival experience.([curseforge.com][1])

This system not only increases the survival challenge but also adds complexity and enjoyment to food management.
